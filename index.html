The Minute Everything Changed

I read your poem. I read it over and over again, feeling every single piece of your heart that you poured into it.

I know your mind feels heavy right now. I know you are feeling low. But I want you to stop, close your eyes for a second, and remember the warmth of that exact moment on the 13th.

You are safe here with me.

I Remember It All

I may have said "I love you" first, but you were the one who made my world softer.

I cherish every sunset date where time moved too fast. I cherish the dairy dates, the shared bites, and holding your hand while you bravely learned to ride that scooty.

You watched my reactions during movie nights, but I was just mesmerized that someone like you chose me.

Our Dream House

Whatever storm you are facing today, it is temporary. Because our future is permanent.

I am going to give you those sleepy mornings. We are going to have a kitchen filled with my favorite food and our terrible singing.

I already hear the tiny footsteps. I can already picture the pets curled up beside us.

Loving you feels like coming home. And I am never leaving.

My Vow

I know today is hard. But let me carry the weight for you today.

You said you'll love me forever, but I promise I will spend every ordinary day and every beautiful moment making sure you never doubt it.

I love you more than you'll ever know. ❤️

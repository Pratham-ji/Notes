Growth Plan — Small daily actions

Maturity is practiced, not declared. Pick 1–2 tasks each day. Check them off; they persist locally.

Examples: Active listening for 5 minutes, send one reassuring message, own one mistake and say how you'll change it, plan a small shared activity.

Psychology-backed tips — be steady and kind

  1. Listen with attention. Put your phone away for 3–5 minutes. Reflect back what she says: “I hear that you felt…”
  2. Own small actions. Apologise concisely for your action: “I’m sorry I did X. I will do Y instead.” This increases trust.
  3. Consistency beats drama. Small predictable kindnesses (a message, a note) are more healing than grand gestures.
  4. Ask, don’t fix. If she’s upset, ask “Do you want solutions or just to be heard?” Respect the answer.
  5. Self-regulate. Practice a calm breathing pause before responding — names: box breathing or 4-4-4 helps.

These are simple, practical steps — used over time they rebuild safety and connection.
